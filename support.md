## About the App

Incident Management Tablet allows users to create and manage incidents, operational periods, personnel, resources, radio information, medical resources, and ICS documentation.

The app supports completion and PDF generation of multiple ICS forms, including ICS 204, ICS 205, ICS 206, ICS 208, ICS 213, ICS 213-RR, ICS 214, and ICS 225-WF.

## Privacy

Incident and operational data is stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export or share a document.

For additional information, see the Incident Management Tablet Privacy Policy.
